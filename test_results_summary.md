# Empirical Evaluation & Test Results Summary
## Kid-Friendly Video Text Classifier

> **Model Architecture**: Classical NLP (TF-IDF Unigrams + Bigrams) + Regularized Logistic Regression  
> **Benchmark Dataset**: [SafeWatch-Bench-200K](https://huggingface.co/datasets/Zhaorun/SafeWatch-Bench-200K-720P) (Strict Non-Sexual Filtering)  
> **Test Split Size**: 2,500 held-out video scene descriptions (1,250 SAFE / 1,250 UNSAFE)  
> **Inference Strategy**: Sentence-by-Sentence Early-Exit Scanning  

---

## 1. Executive Summary

This document provides a comprehensive analysis of our custom **Kid-Friendly Video Content Moderation Classifier**. Designed to interface directly with video descriptions generated by multimodal vision models (such as Google Gemini), our model evaluates scene-by-scene narrative descriptions and outputs an unequivocal binary determination: **`SAFE` (Kid-Friendly)** or **`UNSAFE` (Not Kid-Friendly)**.

On an independent, stratified test dataset of **2,500 video descriptions** that the model had never encountered during training, the model achieved an exceptional **`91.92%` test accuracy** with a balanced **`91.91%` macro F1-score** and **`96.54%` Area Under the ROC Curve (ROC-AUC)**.

## 6. Sentence-by-Sentence Early-Exit Methodology

When deployed in production alongside multimodal vision models (e.g., Google Gemini), video scene summaries can span dozens of sentences. Our system applies **Sentence-by-Sentence Early-Exit Scanning**:

1. **Atomic Sentence Parsing**: Gemini's comprehensive text summary is tokenized into discrete grammatical sentences.
2. **Sequential Inference**: The classifier scans sentences sequentially from start to finish.
3. **Immediate Halt on Violation**: **If any single sentence contains an unsafe action, physical altercation, or hazard, scanning immediately terminates.**
4. **Computational Efficiency**: The system does not waste processing time reading the remainder of the text once an infraction is detected.
5. **Strict Child Safety**: A video containing 9 peaceful scenes and 1 violent scene is properly certified **`UNSAFE`** instantly.
